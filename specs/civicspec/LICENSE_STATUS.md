# License status

No project license has been selected for this initial publication. Public repository access does not itself grant an open-source license. The owner should explicitly choose terms for original documentation, code, schemas, and contributed material before inviting broad downstream redistribution.

Historical research files retain their original content and provenance. They are not a blanket relicensing of referenced standards, laws, or third-party civic records. External standards are linked rather than reproduced. Examples under `examples/` are synthetic constructions, not records of actual meetings.

Licensing is an open maintainer decision tracked as ROADMAP item R01. A contribution proposing terms should explain the intended reuse of documentation, software, schemas, and source examples separately. Do not infer copyright ownership or permissions from a source URL alone.
