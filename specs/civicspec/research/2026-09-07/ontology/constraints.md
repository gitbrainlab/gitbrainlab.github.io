# Constraint boundaries

The Turtle SHACL shapes express an initial graph integrity contract. Their syntax was parsed; no SHACL engine was available in this run. `scripts/competency_tests.py` executes a documented subset of checks independently.

Structural validity is not truth, and is not a legal-compliance verdict. A coherent vote graph can still describe an incorrectly extracted vote. A signed source hash authenticates bytes, not the claims in them. Current statutes and rules cannot automatically determine the applicable historical regime.

Promotion gates: an assertion must have a pinned source version, exact selector, qualified subject and predicate, an extraction/review activity, and an explicit review state. Material amounts, votes, contested wording, signatory identities, legal obligations and publication dates require original-source review. Source-reported legal conclusions remain attributed. Unknowns are not false, and gaps are not misconduct.

Decision state is compositional: proposed text, motion outcome, legal effect, instrument execution, funding, and physical completion are separate properties. No single `approved=true` controls all of them. An authority's “shall” can create a reported obligation without demonstrating performance. Conditions and contingencies attach to the obligation or authorization they qualify.

Field coverage and controlled vocabulary coverage are separately measured. A known `action.kind` predicate carrying a previously unseen code does not establish that a parser recognizes that action. Unmapped fields stay in the source IR and an explicit queue, not a silently enlarged ontology or undifferentiated description.
